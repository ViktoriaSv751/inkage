**Mintaválasz (2.):**
> Yes, fine line work softens faster than bold work, because a single needle line is close to the width that ink naturally spreads in the skin over the years. A crisp hairline on day one usually reads visibly softer and wider after a decade. What helps most: going a little larger than you planned, keeping letters and gaps at least 1 mm apart, a placement that gets little sun and friction (inner upper arm rather than fingers), and daily SPF once it's healed. I wrote up what each decade typically does to fine line work here: https://inkage.app/tudastar/fine-line-tattoos-aging?lang=en
